Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026.

This policy covers all Steel Muse Inc. websites.

Analytics and your choices

With your consent, we use Google Analytics to understand visits and interactions with our websites and improve them. It uses cookies and sends usage and device information to Google. Analytics stays off unless you accept; declining does not limit access.

Use Privacy Choices in the footer to change your decision. Your choice is saved for up to 180 days and applies separately to each browser and website. Withdrawing consent stops future analytics collection; it does not erase information already collected. Analytics cookies are configured for 180 days without automatic renewal.

See Google's Analytics data practices for details of Google's processing. Information may be processed outside Canada.

Browser preferences

Where indicated, we save reading preferences in your browser, separately from analytics. Website search, where implemented, runs in your browser. You can clear saved preferences and consent choices through your browser's site-data controls.

Website services

Our hosting providers process connection information to deliver and secure the websites. Pages using Google Fonts or embedded YouTube videos also connect to Google, independently of your analytics choice. See Google's Privacy Policy for those services' practices.

Contact and personal information

If you email us, we use the information you provide to respond. We do not sell or rent personal information.

For privacy questions or requests to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold, contact hello@steelmuse.com.

We will update this policy when our practices change.
